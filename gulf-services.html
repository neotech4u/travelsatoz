Gulf Visit Visa FAQ

What documents are needed for a Gulf visit visa? ▼
A clear passport scan (front & back) with at least 6 months validity and a passport-size photograph with a white background are the standard requirements for GCC tourist visas.
How long does a UAE tourist visa take? ▼
Standard UAE tourist visas are usually processed within 48 to 72 hours. Express services can often deliver the visa in less than 24 hours.
Can families visit Saudi Arabia on a tourist visa? ▼
Yes, Saudi Arabia's tourist visa allows families to visit and even perform Umrah. However, for longer family stays, the specific "Family Visit Visa" is recommended for religious and social ease.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a fresh passport take in Tamil Nadu? ▼
Standard applications usually take 15-20 days including police verification. Tatkal applications can be ready in 1-3 working days.
Can you book group air tickets for Gulf travel? ▼
Yes, we specialize in group bookings for families and commercial teams travelling to UAE, Saudi, and Qatar.
What documents are needed for a 30-day Dubai visit visa? ▼
You typically need a clear passport copy (front & back) and a passport-size photograph with a white background.
Are Umrah packages inclusive of food? ▼
We offer both Economy and Deluxe packages. Deluxe packages usually include full catering services, while Economy focuses on travel essentials. Please note that laundry services are not included.
Which TN government certificates can you help with? ▼
We assist with Income, Community, Nativity, and First Graduate certificates through the official e-District portal.
Do you assist with GAMCA medical appointments? ▼
Yes, we handle the registration and appointment scheduling for GAMCA medical check-ups required for Gulf work visas.

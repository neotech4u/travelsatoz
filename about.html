Who We Are

Born in Kadayanallur,
Built for Our Community

A to Z Travels & Umrah Service was founded in 2021 with a clear mission: to bring world-class travel and documentation services to the people of Kadayanallur and surrounding areas at honest, affordable prices.

We understand the unique needs of Tamil Muslim families — whether it's visiting relatives in the Gulf, performing a holy pilgrimage, or handling government paperwork. Our team speaks your language and cares for your journey as if it were our own.

"Our priority is not just to book a ticket, but to ensure every pilgrim and traveler feels supported and safe from our doorstep to their destination."
